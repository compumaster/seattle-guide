AMP Annual Meeting 2026 · Seattle Convention Center · November 10–14, 2026
A handpicked selection of the Emerald City's best dining, shopping, and walkable attractions — all within easy reach of the Seattle Convention Center. Pike Place Market and the Seattle Art Museum (SAM) are listed separately as featured highlights.
Where to Eat A range of budgets — walking times from the convention center
  • Beecher's Handmade Cheese $ · Pike Place · 10 min
    "World's Best" mac & cheese; watch cheese made through the window.
  • Pike Place Chowder $ · Post Alley · 10 min
    Award-winning New England clam chowder and bisques.
  • Bombo Italian Kitchen $$ · On-site · 0 min
    Pacific NW–inspired Italian right in the Summit building.
  • Wild Ginger $$ · 3 min
    Acclaimed Pan-Asian institution with a satay bar and curries.
  • Din Tai Fung $$ · Pacific Place · 5 min
    World-famous soup dumplings and Taiwanese specialties.
  • The Pink Door $$$ · Post Alley · 10 min
    Italian dining with bay views and live cabaret. Reservations essential.
  • Matt's in the Market $$$ · 10 min
    Farm-to-table seafood gem overlooking Pike Place Market.
  • Metropolitan Grill $$$ · 10 min
    Seattle's premier steakhouse since 1983 — USDA Prime.
Walkable Attractions Plus featured: Pike Place Market & Seattle Art Museum (SAM)
  • Seattle Waterfront & Pier 57 Free · 12 min
    Renovated waterfront with the Great Wheel, shops, and Elliott Bay views.
  • Pioneer Square Free · 15 min
    Seattle's oldest neighborhood — galleries, historic architecture, underground tours.
  • Olympic Sculpture Park Free · 18 min
    Free outdoor sculpture park with waterfront and mountain panoramas.
  • Space Needle ★ $$$ · 20 min (Monorail 2 min)
    The 520-ft revolving glass floor. Combo ticket with Chihuly ($69) is best value.
  • Chihuly Garden & Glass ★ $$$ · 20 min
    Breathtaking blown-glass sculptures indoors and in the garden, beside the Space Needle.
Shopping Seven standout districts & destinations
  • Pacific Place Shopping Mall · 3 min
    Upscale downtown mall with dining, cinema, and retail.
  • Nordstrom Flagship Department Store · 4 min
    The original flagship — Nordstrom was founded in Seattle in 1901.
  • Westlake Center Shopping Center · 5 min
    Downtown retail and the Monorail terminal.
  • Pike Place Market Shops Artisan / Local · 10 min
    Handcrafted jewelry, local art, flowers, and Seattle souvenirs.
  • Elliott Bay Book Company Independent Bookstore · 15 min
    Multi-level indie bookstore — Capitol Hill's literary soul.
  • University Village Open-Air Shopping · 15 min drive
    Upscale open-air center with national brands and local boutiques.
  • Bellevue Square Shopping Mall · via 2 Line rail
    Premium Eastside shopping, reached on the new 2 Line light rail.
